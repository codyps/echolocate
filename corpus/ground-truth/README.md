These are correlated channels, not six independent sessions. No geographic origin or UTC is published, and source time conventions remain conditional. See [DLR source receipts](dlr-rec139/README.md) and [actual estimates](../../docs/LOCAL_LOCATION_VALIDATION.md).

The original creator-linked recording contributes a continuous 18-minute excerpt. Primary on-screen evidence verifies the date as 2026-09-08 and identifies the Oyster Bay camera site. The creator's map search pin is an approximate reference, not surveyed microphone truth; exact waveform UTC remains unverified. See [source evidence](colsto/README.md).

AeroSonicDB provides four longer propeller recordings, but its public coordinates are intentionally displaced and filename timezone is unstated. These are unsuitable for exact location-error claims. Their CC BY-NC license is retained; the audio is excluded from the public static bundle. See [source metadata](aerosonic/README.md).
